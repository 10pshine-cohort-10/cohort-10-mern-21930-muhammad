### Priority Issues

- **[P1] What**: False `[ DRAFT ]` / spurious autosave on opening existing non-empty note & silent autosave errors
- **Why it matters**: Post-mount state population in `NoteEditorDrawer` triggered false diffs after 1.5s while failed autosaves cleared silently.
- **Fix**: Initialize `NoteEditorDrawer` state synchronously from `note`, wire `onError` into `saveError` alert banner, and guard un-flushed existing edits on close. *(Applied and unit-tested after critique.)*
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

- **[P1] What**: Global `⌘K` Command Palette omitted outside Dashboard/Scratchpad & unhandled async errors on Archive/Dashboard batch actions
- **Why it matters**: Power users pressing `⌘K` on Progress/Archive/Profile got no response, while `ArchivePage` masked fetch errors as empty states and Dashboard batch mutations dropped errors.
- **Fix**: Mount fallback `CommandPalette` in `Layout.tsx`, render `errorMessage` + `Retry` in `ArchivePage.tsx`, and wrap batch/archive/board mutations in `try/catch` alerts. *(Applied and verified after critique.)*
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

- **[P2] What**: Board keyboard-nav leak, `:focus:not(:focus-visible)` CSS override, `#archive` leakage in `NoteTagList`/`getCachedFolders`/`logout`, and mobile hit targets
- **Why it matters**: Invisible `J`/`K`/`D` nav could delete hidden notes in Board view, native `<select>`/`<input>` lost keyboard focus outlines, and `#archive` leaked into local tags/caches.
- **Fix**: Disable `useDeskKeyboardNav` in `board` view, scope focus suppression to `:focus:not(:focus-visible)`, reject `archive` in `NoteTagList` / filter `getCachedFolders` / clear tag caches on `logout`, disable Archive on guest drafts, and enforce coarse `44×44px` hit areas. *(Applied and verified after critique.)*
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`
