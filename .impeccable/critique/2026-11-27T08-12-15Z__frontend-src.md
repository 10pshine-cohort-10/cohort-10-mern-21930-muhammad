### Priority Issues

- **[P0] What**: `MobileNavDock` (`z-index: 100`) covers `NoteEditor` footer (`Save Note` / `Cancel`) on mobile (`<= 768px`)
- **Why it matters**: Mobile users cannot reliably tap Save/Cancel inside the editor drawer.
- **Fix**: Lower `.dock` z-index to `var(--z-floating)` and portal `NoteEditor` to `document.body`.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`

- **[P0] What**: `useDebouncedAutosave` skips TipTap body edits AND enters an infinite save loop on title/tag edits
- **Why it matters**: Body edits are lost on backdrop close while metadata edits hammer `PUT /api/notes/:id` every `1.5s`.
- **Fix**: Store `onSave` in a `useRef`, wire TipTap `onUpdate` `contentVersion`, skip identical snapshots, and confirm discarding non-empty new drafts on backdrop click.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

- **[P0] What**: `DashboardPage` `J/K` + `D` acts on the wrong note when pinned notes are present
- **Why it matters**: `useDeskKeyboardNav` indexes `displayedNotes` instead of rendered `[...pinnedNotes, ...regularNotes]`, deleting the wrong card.
- **Fix**: Pass `orderedNotes = [...pinnedNotes, ...regularNotes]` to `useDeskKeyboardNav` and finalize pending `useSoftDelete` deletes cleanly on unmount.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

- **[P1] What**: No UI action anywhere to Archive a note, yet `Archive` is a primary navigation destination
- **Why it matters**: Users cannot archive notes without manually typing `#archive`, while `AddFolderModal` allows creating `#archive` which vanishes.
- **Fix**: Add Archive/Unarchive actions on `NoteCard`, `NoteEditorHeader`, and `selectionBar` (plus batch Delete), and forbid reserved `archive` in `AddFolderModal`.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable clarify`

- **[P1] What**: Undefined `--shadow-pin-btn`, `UndoToast` overlap, mode fractures, and mobile/a11y regressions
- **Why it matters**: Breaks tactile ink shadows, obscures dock/quick-add, strips `status` in `guestStorage`/`guestSync`, shrinks 30d SVG chart labels to `~4.6px`, leaves nested buttons in `NoteCard`, fails `3.42:1` on `.pinActive`, and exposes mobile auth clip-path overlap on form errors.
- **Fix**: Define `--shadow-pin-btn` in `global.css`, reposition `UndoToast`, preserve `status` & folder context, enable mobile Quick Add & chart horizontal scroll, fix nested `<button>` in `NoteCard`, add `aria-label` on collapsed sidebar links, constrain mobile auth art height, and calibrate `--accent-orange-text` to `>=4.5:1` across all surfaces.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`
