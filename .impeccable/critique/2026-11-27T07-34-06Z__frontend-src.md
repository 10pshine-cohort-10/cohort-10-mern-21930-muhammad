### Overall Impression

A thoughtful tactile card engine and calm, guilt-free Progress dashboard undermined by an unintegrated brand switchover, a buried Kanban Board, and rough edge-case ergonomics (destructive archive clicks, broken keyboard promises, missing mobile folders, and uncalibrated pastel `--text-subtle` contrast). Unifying around **Pinnate** gives the entire platform its missing center of gravity.

### What's Working

1. **Non-punitive Progress narrative (`InsightsPage.tsx`, `ActivityBarChart.tsx`, `PlanProgressRing.tsx`)**: Blue activity bars (`7d`/`30d`), orange completion arc (`Done`), and factual reassuring copy reject streak pressure cleanly.
2. **Empathetic Auth flow (`AuthSplitLayout.tsx`, `AuthHandDrawnArt.tsx`, `authValidation.ts`)**: Single-screen clip morph, email preservation across route toggles, 72-byte bcrypt validation, reduced-motion static support, and focus/typing pause show rare restraint.
3. **Physical card & organic motion grammar (`NoteCard.module.css`, `KanbanCard.tsx`, `LandingScrollThread.tsx`, `FogTransitionOverlay.tsx`)**: Offset card ink outlines, deterministic tilts, and blue-to-orange thread/leaf continuity establish a distinct tactile feel.

### Priority Issues

- **[P0] What**: Unfinished Pinnate brand & vocabulary split keeps the wrong product in focus
- **Why it matters**: Users see `Notes App` / `Notes Workspace` (`BookOpen`), `"Quick tack"`, `"Tack to notes"`, and `"desk"` jargon, while defaulting to a flat `All Notes` grid where `Board` is hidden behind a toggle and `Quick tack` / `Scratchpad` items (`status: null`) trigger `"Your plan board is clear"` and `0%` Progress.
- **Fix**: Single-pass rename to **Pinnate** (`PIN-ayt`) + custom leaf mark (`PinnateMark` + favicon), replace `"tack"`/`"desk"`/`"Notes Workspace"` with `"Add to notes"` / `"Board"` / `"Stage"` / `"Pin"`, show stage indicators on cards, allow stage assignment from quick capture / Scratchpad promotion, and elevate Board visibility.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable clarify`

- **[P1] What**: Broken keyboard, modal, command palette, and heading contracts
- **Why it matters**: `KeyboardHelpModal` promises `T` (`QuickTackBar` binds `Q` while unmounting when collapsed) and `Esc` (no Escape listener); `LandingFeatureTabs` advertises `⌘K` actions absent from `CommandPalette.tsx`; `DashboardPage` & `ArchivePage` lack `<h1>` in `<main>` (breaking `PageTransitionContext` heading focus).
- **Fix**: Standardize quick-add hotkey + expand behavior, wire `Escape` & `?` trigger on `KeyboardHelpModal`, add view/folder navigation to `CommandPalette.tsx`, and render semantic `<h1>` landmarks on all routes.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

- **[P1] What**: Unconfirmed destructive actions and async state-loss hazards
- **Why it matters**: `ArchivePage` permanently deletes or empties archive in 1 click without confirmation/undo; `useSoftDelete` loses the first note on rapid 2nd delete within 5s; `AuthContext` calls `syncGuestNotesIfAny` unawaited; `NoteEditor` omits `status` from `useDebouncedAutosave` and swallows manual save errors in `console.error`.
- **Fix**: Add inline/modal confirmation on permanent archive deletes, flush/stack pending soft deletes cleanly, `await` guest note sync with status feedback, include `status` in `useDebouncedAutosave`, and render save error banners in `NoteEditor`.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

- **[P1] What**: Mobile drops `#folders`, relies on icon-only nav, and crowds the bottom thumb zone
- **Why it matters**: `Layout.module.css` hides `.sidebar` at `<=768px` with zero folder UI elsewhere; `MobileNavDock` uses unlabeled `36×36px` icon buttons directly below the floating `Quick tack` pill at `bottom: 76px`, duplicating the center `+` CTA.
- **Fix**: Add text micro-labels and `>=44×44px` hit areas to `MobileNavDock`, surface a compact horizontal `#folder` bar on mobile Dashboard, and deduplicate mobile `+` vs `Quick add`.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`

- **[P2] What**: Semantic color/contrast leaks, Dashboard filter pollution, and component duplication
- **Why it matters**: `--text-subtle` (`#71717a`) fails `4.5:1` live on `.pinnedCtaMeta` (`4.49:1`) and across all 5 pastels; `KanbanCard` puts red pins on unpinned cards while `#ef4444` is also used in `ErrorBoundary`; `DashboardPage` counts archived notes in `totalNotes` and lists `#archive` in `#Folders`; `LandingHero` triggers `nested-cards` and `LandingPage` leaks orange onto decorative CTA tape.
- **Fix**: Separate `--pin-red` from `--danger-text`, darken `--text-subtle` for `>=4.5:1` on pastels, render pins only when `note.isPinned === true`, filter `'archive'` out of Dashboard totals/folders, remove redundant `KanbanCard` move UI, and flatten `.deskStage` card nesting.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`
